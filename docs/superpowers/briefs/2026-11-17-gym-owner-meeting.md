# Meeting brief: pilot gym owner

One page to take into the meeting. Fill the blanks before you go.

## The offer in one sentence

Your gym's own app: your trainers set each client's workout and watch it happen from a tablet, and
your members get a simple app with your name on it that only does what they need.

## What he gets

**For his trainers**
- A list of their clients with who trained, who missed a session, who hit a record.
- "Marco, pull today": pick the workout for a client in two taps. The client's phone shows it.
- A live board of the gym floor: each client's exercise, set and rest. No more walking over to ask
  how many sets are left.
- Corrections to a client's plan or logged workout, which the client can see and undo.

**For his members**
- An app under the gym's name, logo and colours, on their home screen.
- Open it, see today's workout from their trainer, press Start.
- Their history and progress. Nothing else unless the gym switches it on.

**For him**
- Every trainer and member in one view. He decides what the members' app shows.
- The gym's data sits on its own server, separate from every other gym.
- Members agree once, in plain words, to what trainers can see, and can change it later.

## What is real today and what is coming

Be exact about this. He has 30 years of experience and will notice a stretch.

| Works now | Needs building |
|---|---|
| Trainer accounts and client lists | The gym's own branding (phase 1) |
| Trainer builds routines and assigns them to clients | A stripped-down member app the gym controls (phase 2) |
| Trainer dashboard with flags per client | "Train this today" in two taps, gym assigns clients to trainers (phase 3) |
| Trainer edits a client's plan, client can undo | The live floor board (phase 4) |
| Members join with a code or QR | Coach changes a set while the client trains; a store app (phase 5) |

Rough guesses for one developer: a pilot that carries his brand and covers phases 1 to 3 is about
5 to 7 weeks of building. The live board is about 2 more.

## Do not promise

- An App Store or Play listing under his gym's name. The plan is a home-screen web app with his
  brand first, then one store app under our name that takes on his brand after sign-in.
- The exercise animations in a rebranded app, until our own media licence is signed.
- Dates. Give ranges.
- Billing, door access, class booking or gym announcements. Those are parked.

## Questions to ask him

1. How do your trainers plan a client's session today? Paper, a spreadsheet, memory, another app?
2. How many trainers, and how many clients each? How many members in total?
3. One-to-one training, small groups, classes, or all three?
4. Who decides which trainer a member gets: you, the front desk, or the member?
5. Would trainers carry a tablet on the floor, or use their phone?
6. What do members use now to follow their programme? What do they complain about?
7. Do you already run membership or booking software? Would this need to live beside it?
8. What would make this worth paying for, and who pays: the gym, the trainer, or the member?
9. What did you like in the pilot you saw, and what was missing?
10. Would you run a pilot with two or three trainers and their clients for a month?

## What we need from him to start

- Logo as PNG (square, and a wide version) and the brand colour.
- The name the app should carry, and whether he has a web domain we can use a subdomain of.
- Two or three trainers willing to try it, with about ten clients each.
- One person at the gym who is our contact for feedback.

## The ask

A one-month pilot at his gym, starting once phases 1 to 3 are built. In return for his feedback
and his name as the first reference gym: ________ (decide your terms before the meeting).
